# Privacy Policy for Meme Creator

Last updated: Mai 19, 2025

## Introduction

Welcome to Meme Creator. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you about how we handle your data when you use our application.

## Information We Collect

### User-Provided Content
- Images you select from your device to create memes
- Text you add to create memes

### Automatically Collected Information
- AdMob advertising identifiers
- Basic device information (device type, operating system version)

## How We Use Your Information

1. **Meme Creation**: We use the images and text you provide solely for the purpose of creating memes within the app.

2. **Advertising**: We use AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process data according to their privacy policy.

3. **App Functionality**: We use device information to ensure the app functions properly on your device.

## Data Storage

- All meme creation happens locally on your device
- We do not store your images or text on our servers
- Created memes are saved to your device's storage with your permission
