KINOVA – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026 · App version 1.1.0 · Deutsch · Français · العربية

1. Who is responsible

Khalid Ramze, Schafheckstraße 6, 60599 Frankfurt, Germany, e-mail: khalidramze.1995@gmail.com (“we”). This policy covers the Android app “KINOVA – Move better. Become stronger.” (package app.kinova.fitness).

2. Your fitness data stays on your device

KINOVA has no user accounts and we run no servers. Everything you enter – your profile (goal, level, age, optional sex, weight, height, training days), daily check-ins (water, meals and optional calories, workouts, rest days, mood), weight history, badges, streaks, reminder settings and app settings (including your language choice) – is stored only locally in the app’s private storage on your device. We never receive, see or sell this data. The app works offline.

Automatic Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for KINOVA, so this data is not copied to Google Drive either. Uninstalling the app or using Settings › Reset all data deletes it permanently.

KINOVA is a fitness and wellness app. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnoses or treatment. Calorie, protein and water targets are general estimates.

3. Progress photos

If you choose to add progress photos, you pick them with the Android photo picker. The app copies the selected image into its private storage on your device. Photos are never uploaded or shared, and other apps cannot access them. You can delete individual photos in the app; all photos are removed when you reset data or uninstall. The app does not request camera or storage permissions.

4. Notifications

If you enable reminders (water, workout, evening check-in), the app asks for the Android notification permission and schedules local notifications on your device with Android WorkManager. No data is sent anywhere for this. You can turn reminders off in the app or in Android settings at any time.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). Ads are shown as banners outside of workouts, occasionally as a full-screen ad after a finished workout, and as an optional rewarded ad that you can watch to unlock an advanced plan for 24 hours. To deliver, measure and – if you agree – personalise ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers (advertising ID, app set ID)Advertising, frequency capping, analytics, fraud prevention
Approximate location (derived from your IP address)Advertising (e.g. regional ads), fraud prevention
App interactions (ad impressions and clicks, app launches)Advertising measurement, analytics
Diagnostics (crash and performance data of the ads SDK)Analytics, keeping ads working

Your fitness, nutrition, mood, weight and photo data are never passed to the ads SDK or to Google. Data is transmitted encrypted (HTTPS). Google acts as an independent controller for this processing; see Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. Data may be transferred to the USA; Google relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses.

Ads are limited to content rated at most PG (parental guidance).

6. Consent (Google User Messaging Platform)

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before any ad is requested; no ad is requested before the consent process has finished. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25 TDDDG for access to device information). If you decline, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads that use data only as necessary (e.g. fraud prevention, frequency capping). You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the app under Settings › Ads & privacy › Privacy options. Outside these regions, ads are based on our legitimate interest in financing the free app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and on applicable local law.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads).

7. In-app purchase “KINOVA Pro” (Google Play Billing)

KINOVA Pro is a one-time purchase that removes ads and unlocks all advanced plans. Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details; the app only asks Google Play whether you own the purchase (also when you tap Restore purchase). See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

8. Permissions

Internet and network state (ads and purchases only), advertising ID (ads), notifications (reminders, only if you enable them), vibration (rest-timer feedback). No location, camera, microphone, contacts or health-sensor permissions are used.

9. Age

KINOVA is intended for adults (18+). It is not directed at children.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, objection and withdrawal of consent, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (e.g. Der Hessische Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit). Because your fitness data exists only on your device, you control it directly in the app. Requests about ad data are best directed to Google (Google privacy controls); we are happy to help: khalidramze.1995@gmail.com.

11. Changes

We will update this policy when the app changes. The current version is always available at this address.
