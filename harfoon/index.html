Harfoon (حرفون) – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026 · App version 1.0.0 · العربية · Deutsch · Français

1. Who is responsible

Khalid Ramze, Germany, e-mail: khalidramze.1995@gmail.com (“we”). This policy covers the Android app “Harfoon: Arabic word game” (package app.harfoon.words).

2. We collect no personal data

Harfoon has no accounts, no sign-up and we run no servers. Your games, statistics, streaks, hints, achievements, pack progress and settings are stored only on your device in the app’s private storage. We never receive, see or sell this data. The game works fully offline. Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled for this app. Uninstalling the app deletes the data.

When you share a result or a challenge, the app hands a text/image to the app you pick (e.g. WhatsApp) through Android’s share function; we do not receive it. A challenge code only contains the encoded secret word. When you install via a challenge link, Google Play passes that code to the app (Install Referrer); it is only used to open the challenge.

3. Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks for the notification permission and schedules a local notification with Android WorkManager. No data is sent anywhere. You can turn it off at any time.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland): a small banner on menu screens, occasionally a full-screen ad between practice or pack rounds (never during the daily puzzle), and optional rewarded ads you can watch for a hint. To deliver, measure and – if you agree – personalise ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers (advertising ID, app set ID)Advertising, frequency capping, analytics, fraud prevention
Approximate location (derived from IP address)Advertising, fraud prevention
App interactions (ad views and clicks, app launches)Advertising measurement, analytics
Diagnostics (crash/performance data of the ads SDK)Analytics, keeping ads working

Your game data is never passed to the ads SDK. Data is transmitted encrypted. Google acts as an independent controller; see Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps. Data may be transferred to the USA (EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, Standard Contractual Clauses). Ads are limited to content rated PG at most.

5. Consent (Google User Messaging Platform)

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent via Google’s UMP before ads are requested (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25 TDDDG). If you decline, Google may still show non-personalised/limited ads. You can change your choice any time in Settings › Ad privacy options. Elsewhere ads are based on our legitimate interest in financing the free app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

6. In-app purchase “Remove ads”

“Remove ads” (product remove_ads_pro) is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. We receive no payment details; the app only asks Google Play whether you own it. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

7. Permissions

Internet and network state (ads and purchases only), advertising ID (ads), notifications (reminder, only if enabled), vibration (feedback). No location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions.

8. Age

Harfoon is intended for users aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, withdrawal of consent and to complain to a supervisory authority. Your game data exists only on your device, so you control it directly. For ad data see Google privacy controls; we are happy to help: khalidramze.1995@gmail.com.

10. Changes

We will update this policy when the app changes. The current version is always at this address.
