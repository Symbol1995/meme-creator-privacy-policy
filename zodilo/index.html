Zodilo – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026 · App version 1.0.0 · Deutsch · Français · العربية

1. Who is responsible

Khalid Ramze, Schafheckstraße 6, 60599 Frankfurt, Germany, e-mail: khalidramze.1995@gmail.com (“we”). This policy covers the Android app “Zodilo – Horoscope & Love Compatibility” (package app.zodilo.astro).

2. Your names and birth dates stay on your device

Zodilo has no user accounts and we run no servers. Everything you enter – your first name and birth date, the names and birth dates you use in compatibility tests, your saved match history, unlocked readings, reminder and app settings (including your language) – is stored only locally in the app’s private storage on your device. Horoscopes, sign profiles, Chinese zodiac, life path numbers and compatibility scores are calculated offline on your device. We never receive, see or sell this data.

Automatic Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for Zodilo, so this data is not copied to Google Drive either. Uninstalling the app deletes it permanently.

Please only enter other people’s names and birthdays if they are fine with it. Nothing you enter leaves your phone unless you decide to share a card yourself.

3. Share cards

When you tap Share, the app draws an image (e.g. your match result) on your device, saves it temporarily in the app’s private cache and hands it to the Android share sheet. Only the app you choose (e.g. a messenger) receives the image or text – and only because you decided to share it. We receive nothing.

4. Notifications and widget

If you enable the daily horoscope reminder, the app asks for the Android notification permission and schedules a local notification with Android WorkManager. The home-screen widget is updated locally as well. No data is sent anywhere for this. You can turn the reminder off in the app or in Android settings at any time.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland): banners on overview screens, occasionally a full-screen ad after you have seen a compatibility result, and optional rewarded ads that you can watch to unlock a detailed, weekly or monthly reading. To deliver, measure and – if you agree – personalise ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers (advertising ID, app set ID)Advertising, frequency capping, analytics, fraud prevention
Approximate location (derived from your IP address)Advertising (e.g. regional ads), fraud prevention
App interactions (ad impressions and clicks, app launches)Advertising measurement, analytics
Diagnostics (crash and performance data of the ads SDK)Analytics, keeping ads working

Names, birth dates, zodiac signs and compatibility results are never passed to the ads SDK or to Google. Data is transmitted encrypted (HTTPS). Google acts as an independent controller for this processing; see Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. Data may be transferred to the USA; Google relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses.

Ads are limited to content rated at most PG (parental guidance).

6. Consent (Google User Messaging Platform)

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before any ad is requested; no ad is requested before the consent process has finished. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25 TDDDG for access to device information). If you decline, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads that use data only as necessary (e.g. fraud prevention, frequency capping). You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the app under Me › Settings › Ad privacy choices. Outside these regions, ads are based on our legitimate interest in financing the free app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and on applicable local law.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads).

7. In-app purchase “Remove ads” (Google Play Billing)

“Remove ads” is a one-time purchase that removes banner and full-screen ads and unlocks all readings without rewarded ads. Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details; the app only asks Google Play whether you own the purchase (also when you tap Restore purchase). See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

8. Permissions

Internet and network state (ads and purchases only), advertising ID (ads), notifications (daily reminder, only if you enable it). No location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions are used.

9. Age and nature of the content

Zodilo is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. All horoscopes and compatibility scores are for entertainment only; they are not predictions, advice or facts.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, objection and withdrawal of consent, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (e.g. Der Hessische Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit). Because your entries exist only on your device, you control them directly in the app. Requests about ad data are best directed to Google (Google privacy controls); we are happy to help: khalidramze.1995@gmail.com.

11. Changes

We will update this policy when the app changes. The current version is always available at this address.
