2. Data that stays on your device

Your current game, settings and statistics (wins, streaks, best times) are stored only on your device. We do not run servers and do not receive this data. Android may include it in your personal Google device backup, which Google processes under your Google account. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). To deliver, measure and – if you agree – personalise ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers (advertising ID, app set ID)Advertising, frequency capping, analytics, fraud prevention
Approximate location (derived from your IP address)Advertising (e.g. regional ads), fraud prevention
App interactions (ad impressions and clicks, app launches)Advertising measurement, analytics

Data may be transferred to the USA; Google relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses.

4. Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before ads are requested. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25 TDDDG for access to device information). If you decline, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads, which use data only as necessary (e.g. for fraud prevention and frequency capping). You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the app under Settings › Ads & privacy › Privacy options. Outside these regions, ads are based on our legitimate interest in financing the free app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and on applicable local law.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads), which stops personalised advertising based on it.

5. In-app purchase “Remove ads”

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details; the app only checks with Google Play whether you own the purchase. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice. After purchasing, no more ads are shown.

6. Children

The app is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA). Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences (max rating PG).

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, objection and withdrawal of consent, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. As we hold no personal data on servers, requests about ad data are best directed to Google (Google privacy controls); we are happy to help: khalidramze.1995@gmail.com.

8. Changes

We will update this page if the app’s data practices change; the effective date above shows the latest version.
