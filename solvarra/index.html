Effective date: 4 November 2026 · App version 1.0.0 · Deutsch · Français · العربية

1. Who is responsible

Khalid Ramze, Schafheckstraße 6, 60599 Frankfurt, Germany, e-mail: khalidramze.1995@gmail.com (“we”). This policy covers the Android game “Solvarra: Idle Heroes RPG” (package app.solvarra.idle).

2. Your game progress stays on your device

Solvarra has no user accounts and we run no servers. Your save game – stage, heroes, gear, talents, currencies, quests, achievements, statistics, timers for chests and offline earnings – and your settings (including your language) are stored only locally in the app’s private storage on your device. The whole game runs offline. We never receive, see or sell this data.

Automatic Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for Solvarra, so the save is not copied to Google Drive either. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes your progress permanently.

To keep offline earnings fair, the game compares the device clock with the time since the last boot; this check happens entirely on your device.

3. No sharing, no notifications

Solvarra does not send notifications, does not share content to other apps and does not access your files, contacts, camera, microphone or location.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland): banners on menu screens (never on the battle screen), occasionally a full-screen ad at a calm break (after opening a chest or after a Rekindle – never during a boss fight, at most every few minutes), and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch to double offline earnings, open a bonus chest, get a ×2 damage boost or rally for a retry against a boss. To deliver, measure and – if you agree – personalise ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers (advertising ID, app set ID)Advertising, frequency capping, analytics, fraud prevention
Approximate location (derived from your IP address)Advertising (e.g. regional ads), fraud prevention
App interactions (ad impressions and clicks, app launches)Advertising measurement, analytics
Diagnostics (crash and performance data of the ads SDK)Analytics, keeping ads working

Your game progress is never passed to the ads SDK or to Google. Data is transmitted encrypted (HTTPS). Google acts as an independent controller for this processing; see Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. Data may be transferred to the USA; Google relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses.

Ads are limited to content rated at most PG (parental guidance).

5. Consent (Google User Messaging Platform)

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before any ad is requested; no ad is requested before the consent process has finished. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25 TDDDG for access to device information). If you decline, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads that use data only as necessary (e.g. fraud prevention, frequency capping). You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the app under Quests › Settings › Ad privacy choices. Outside these regions, ads are based on our legitimate interest in financing the free game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and on applicable local law.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads).

6. In-app purchase “Remove ads” (Google Play Billing)

“Remove ads” is a one-time purchase that removes banner and full-screen ads. Rewarded ads remain available as an optional bonus. Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details; the app only asks Google Play whether you own the purchase (also when you tap Restore purchase). See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

7. Permissions

Internet and network state (ads and purchases only) and advertising ID (ads). No location, camera, microphone, contacts, notification or storage permissions are used.

8. Age and nature of the content

Solvarra is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. It contains mild fantasy cartoon combat.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, objection and withdrawal of consent, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (e.g. Der Hessische Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit). Because your save exists only on your device, you control it directly (uninstall or clear app data). Requests about ad data are best directed to Google (Google privacy controls); we are happy to help: khalidramze.1995@gmail.com.

10. Changes

We will update this policy when the app changes. The current version is always available at this address.
