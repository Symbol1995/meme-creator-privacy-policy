Wortsaat – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026 · App version 1.0.0 · Deutsch · العربية

1. Who is responsible

Khalid Ramze, Schafheckstraße 6, 60599 Frankfurt, Germany, e-mail: khalidramze.1995@gmail.com (“we”). This policy covers the Android app “Wortsaat – German for Arabic speakers” (package app.wortsaat.german).

2. Your learning data stays on your device

Wortsaat has no user accounts and we run no servers. Everything you create – your level, daily goal, study progress per word, favourites, “already know” marks, your own words, streaks, XP, badges, reminder settings and app settings (including your language choice) – is stored only locally in the app’s private storage on your device. We never receive, see or sell this data. The app works offline.

Automatic Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled, so this data is not copied to Google Drive either. If you want a backup, you can export a JSON file yourself (Settings › Export progress) and save it wherever you choose using Android’s file picker; the app does not read any other files. Uninstalling the app or using Settings › Reset progress deletes the data permanently.

3. Pronunciation (text-to-speech)

German words are read aloud with the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone (usually Google Speech Services). The app only hands the word to the Android system; no audio or text is sent to us. Depending on the engine you use, voices may be processed on the device or by the engine’s provider under its own privacy policy. The app can open Android’s voice-data installation screen if a German voice is missing.

4. Notifications

If you enable the daily study reminder, the app asks for the Android notification permission and schedules a local notification on your device with Android WorkManager. No data is sent anywhere for this. You can turn reminders off in the app or in Android settings at any time.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). Ads are shown as banners on the Home, Learn and Progress screens (never during a study session), occasionally as a full-screen ad after a finished study session, and as an optional rewarded ad that you can watch to earn a streak freeze. To deliver, measure and – if you agree – personalise ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers (advertising ID, app set ID)Advertising, frequency capping, analytics, fraud prevention
Approximate location (derived from your IP address)Advertising (e.g. regional ads), fraud prevention
App interactions (ad impressions and clicks, app launches)Advertising measurement, analytics
Diagnostics (crash and performance data of the ads SDK)Analytics, keeping ads working

Your learning data, words and progress are never passed to the ads SDK or to Google. Data is transmitted encrypted (HTTPS). Google acts as an independent controller for this processing; see Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. Data may be transferred to the USA; Google relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses.

Ads are limited to content rated at most PG (parental guidance).

6. Consent (Google User Messaging Platform)

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before ads are requested. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25 TDDDG for access to device information). If you decline, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads that use data only as necessary (e.g. fraud prevention, frequency capping). You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the app under Settings › Privacy › Ad privacy options. Outside these regions, ads are based on our legitimate interest in financing the free app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and on applicable local law.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads).

7. In-app purchase “Remove ads” (Google Play Billing)

“Remove ads” (product ID remove_ads_pro) is a one-time purchase that removes all ads. Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details; the app only asks Google Play whether you own the purchase (also when you tap Restore purchase). See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

8. Permissions

Internet and network state (ads and purchases only), advertising ID (ads), notifications (reminders, only if you enable them), vibration (feedback in exercises). No location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions are used.

9. Age

Wortsaat is intended for adults (18+). It is not directed at children.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, objection and withdrawal of consent, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (e.g. Der Hessische Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit). Because your learning data exists only on your device, you control it directly in the app. Requests about ad data are best directed to Google (Google privacy controls); we are happy to help: khalidramze.1995@gmail.com.

11. Changes

We will update this policy when the app changes. The current version is always available at this address.
